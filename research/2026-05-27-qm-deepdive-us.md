## DY — Dycom Industries ($529) | EP, 36% | ★ Highest conviction

**Industry position.** Dominant US specialty contractor for telecom/fiber
buildout — fiber-to-the-home plus a fast-growing data-center electrical arm
(Nov 2025 $1.95B Power Solutions acquisition). Demand is structural: BEAD
federal subsidies + AI-datacenter power/fiber. Cleanest secular tailwind of the four.

**Catalyst (freshest possible).** Q1 FY2027 reported **today, May 27**. Record
revenue **$1.965B**, adj EPS **$4.42 (+85% YoY)**, adj EBITDA margin 13.4%
(+141bps), **book-to-bill 2.2×**, record backlog **$11.9B**. Raised FY27 guide
to **$7.38–7.65B** (from $6.85–7.15B at Q4) and announced a $275M National
Technology Integrators acquisition for data-center capability.

**Management track record.** 4/4 beats, second consecutive guide raise. Long
sandbagging culture — executing the order book, not narrating it.

**Theme vs order book.** Best of the four: $11.9B backlog + 2.2× book-to-bill is
*signed demand*, not a story.

**Bull/bear & valuation.** Bull: backlog visibility + BEAD ramps into 2H-CY2027 =
multi-year runway. Bear: stock is **~11% above the $474 consensus target** (price
has outrun sell-side), reported operating margin thin (4.7%), D/E 1.61, BEAD
revenue back-half-2027 weighted.

**Verdict:** strongest fundamental + momentum combo, but extended post-print. The
disciplined Qullamaggie move is to wait for a tight pullback to the breakout pivot
rather than chase the gap.

## APPS — Digital Turbine ($6.68) | EP, 35% | ⚠ Highest risk/reward

**Industry position.** Mobile app-delivery/ad-tech embedded in carrier and OEM
devices. App Growth Platform (AGP) is the growth engine (+57% YoY, fastest in 3+ years).

**Catalyst (fresh).** Q4 FY2026 reported **yesterday, May 26**. Revenue $142.5M
(+20%), adj EPS $0.16 (beat by $0.08), adj EBITDA +53%, margin to 22%. FY27 guide
$630–650M rev / $135–145M EBITDA, plus new AI tie-ups (Google Cloud, Databricks,
Orange). Stock gapped +18–35%.

**Management track record.** Mixed-to-poor. CEO Bill Stone presided over a ~$90 →
~$2 collapse; guided a return to YoY growth by early 2026 and *did* deliver it —
a credibility rebuild in progress, not established.

**Theme vs order book / sell-side.** AGP reacceleration is real, but the AI
partnerships are early and unmonetized (theme inflation). Only **2 analysts** —
orphaned: under-discovered opportunity *and* no institutional floor.

**Bear case & risks.** $400M debt with **interest expense exceeding operating
income**; dilution risk if monetization slips; **RSI 87.9, +87% in a month** —
extremely extended.

**Verdict:** a legitimate fresh-catalyst EP, but a speculative, balance-sheet-
levered turnaround. Swing-trade only with a tight stop; not buy-and-hold quality yet.

## FCFS — FirstCash Holdings ($231) | Breakout, 35% | Defensive, weakest momentum

**Industry position.** Largest pawn operator (US/Latin America) + AFF lease-to-own
at retail POS (~16,600 merchant locations, +14% YoY). Counter-cyclical — thrives
in consumer stress.

**Catalyst (slightly stale).** Q1 2026 (~Apr 23): record results, revenue +26% to
>$1B, EPS +30%, UK Pawn segment guide raised to $125–135M. Solid — but the scan
tagged it a **breakout on low volume (0.81× avg)**, the weakest momentum signature
of the four, and it lags its sector (RS −8.8).

**Management track record.** Consistent operator, 3/4 beats, raised guidance. Reliable.

**Bull/bear, risks, valuation.** Bull: durable cash-flow compounder, pawn demand
strong. Bear: only **+4.4% to consensus target** (near fair value), insider net
selling, and a **CFPB overhang** — the $11M Military Lending Act settlement
($5–7M redress + $4M fine) is immaterial, but AFF lease-to-own keeps it on the
regulatory radar.

**Verdict:** the best *buy-and-hold* business here, but as a momentum setup it's
the least compelling — limited upside to target, soft volume.

## ADUR — Aduro Clean Technologies ($15.63) | Breakout, 31% | ✗ Speculative, avoid as a "buy"

**Reality check.** Pre-revenue chemical-recycling (hydrochemolytic tech). Pilot
plant in Ontario went operational Feb 2026; first commercial unit targeted at
Chemelot, Netherlands with a **2027 commissioning target gated by permitting**.
The "70% EPS beat" is just a *narrower loss* (−$0.05 vs −$0.17) — meaningless.

**Fails the order-book check outright.** Zero revenue, all narrative. Cash CAD
$39M after a US raise = **active and ongoing dilution**. 3 analysts with a $28.72
target (+84%) — speculative targets on a pre-commercial name.

**Verdict:** a story-stock breakout. It can run on momentum, but it's a binary
venture bet, not an investable thesis. Lottery-ticket sizing only if traded at all.

## Bottom line

**If I had to act:** DY is the standout on fundamentals and order book — but it
gapped on today's print and sits ~11% above consensus target, so the disciplined
move is to wait for a tight pullback to the pivot rather than chase. FCFS is the
one to *own* but not the one to *swing*. APPS is a high-octane swing for
risk-tolerant capital only. ADUR doesn't clear the bar as an investment.
