## Sector 2: Memory / RAM

**Background:** Most cyclical sector in tech. DRAM/NAND collapsed 2022–2023 (prices -50–70%). Now in sharp upcycle driven by: (1) supply discipline, (2) AI HBM demand — every NVIDIA GPU requires High Bandwidth Memory in massive quantities.

The trailing/forward P/E gap reflects trough earnings vs explosive recovery. The market discount is justified — cycles always reverse when manufacturers oversupply. China's CXMT is scaling commodity DRAM with state subsidies, threatening to compress the cycle. MU has structural edge via HBM (only SK Hynix, Micron, Samsung can produce it). Nanya (2408.TW) is pure commodity DRAM — higher risk/reward in both directions.

**Watch signal:** DRAM spot prices on DRAMeXchange. When they peak and roll, exit before guidance cuts.
